Budget Tracker Home-Screen Widgets: How to Add and Use Them on Android

Bramora Labs · Updated October 16, 2026 · 4 min read
Short answer

In Budget Tracker, open Settings, find Home-screen widgets and tap Add spending widget or Add goal widget. You can also long-press your home screen, tap Widgets and pick Budget Tracker. Turn on Hide amounts on widgets if other people can see your phone.

What the widgets show

Budget Tracker has two widgets for Android home screens:

  • Spending widget: how much you have spent this month and, if you set a monthly budget, how much is left.
  • Goal widget: the progress of one savings goal, with the goal name, a progress ring, and the saved and target amounts.

How to add a widget

From inside the app:

  1. Open Budget Tracker and go to Settings.
  2. Find the Home-screen widgets section.
  3. Tap Add spending widget or Add goal widget, then confirm when Android asks to add it.

From your home screen (works on every Android phone):

  1. Long-press an empty area of the home screen.
  2. Tap Widgets and search for Budget Tracker.
  3. Drag the widget you want onto the home screen. Widgets can be resized.

Some phone launchers do not allow apps to add widgets directly. In that case the app shows a short message pointing you to the home-screen method.

Showing more than one goal

Add one goal widget per goal. When you add a new goal widget, it picks a goal that no other goal widget is already showing, so your second widget shows your second goal. If you want a different goal on a widget, check whether your launcher offers a reconfigure option when you long-press it. That option depends on your phone.

Privacy: what a widget can reveal

A widget is part of your home screen, so anyone who can see your screen can see it, including while Budget Tracker itself is locked with a PIN. Budget Tracker has a Hide amounts on widgets switch in Settings that replaces money amounts with dots. Goal names and progress percentages are still shown. The widget’s summary is stored on your device only; read the app privacy policy for details.

Why a widget helps

Seeing the month’s spending without opening an app makes it easier to notice when you are ahead or behind, and a visible goal ring is a gentle daily reminder. For the method behind a goal, see how to save for Umrah, Hajj or a trip home.

Frequently asked questions

Are the widgets free?

Yes. Both the spending widget and the goal widget are free. Only the number of savings goals beyond two needs the one-time Pro purchase.

Can I put two goals on my home screen?

Yes. Add a goal widget for each goal. A new goal widget picks a goal that no other widget is showing, so your second widget shows your second goal. To change which goal a widget shows, use your launcher’s option to reconfigure the widget if it offers one; this varies by phone.

Do widgets work when the app is locked?

The widget shows figures on the home screen even if the app itself is locked with a PIN. That is how Android widgets work. Turn on Hide amounts on widgets in Settings if others may see your screen.

How often do the widgets update?

They update when you change data in the app, and Android also refreshes widgets on its own schedule, which is no more often than about every 30 minutes.
