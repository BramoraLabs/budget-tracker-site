How to Save for Umrah, Hajj or a Trip Home: A Simple Goal-Based Plan

Bramora Labs · Updated October 16, 2026 · 5 min read
Short answer

Pick a target amount and a date, divide what is left by the number of months until then, and treat that monthly amount like a bill. For example, AED 12,000 in 12 months is AED 1,000 a month. Then track what you have actually put aside so the number stays honest.

Step 1: Decide the real target

A goal only works if the number is real. For Umrah or Hajj that means a current quote for the package or the separate costs (flights, accommodation, visa and local transport). For a trip home, price the flights for everyone travelling and add a cushion for gifts and family expenses. Costs change by season, so write down the date you got the quote and refresh it a few months before you travel.

Step 2: Pick a date, not just an amount

Without a date, a goal slides. Choose the month you want to travel and count the months until then. If your date is flexible, set it for the earlier month you would like and let the plan show you what that costs.

Step 3: Divide what is left by the months left

The formula is simple:

Monthly amount = (target − already saved) ÷ months left

A worked example, with made-up round numbers so you can follow the arithmetic:

  • Target for the family trip home: AED 6,500
  • Already put aside: AED 1,500
  • Months until you fly: 10
  • Monthly amount: (6,500 − 1,500) ÷ 10 = AED 500 a month

If you only get part of a month before your date, count it as a full month so you are never behind. Budget Tracker rounds the months up in the same way.

Step 4: Make the monthly amount a fixed line in your budget

Put the monthly goal amount next to rent and remittances, not at the end of the month as “whatever is left”. If you send money home every month, plan it together: see how much to save from a UAE salary for a starting split that accounts for remittances.

Step 5: Track what you actually save

Each time you set money aside, record it against the goal. You see a progress ring, the amount remaining and the monthly figure for the months that are left. The goal screen also marks milestones at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%, which makes a long goal feel less abstract.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I save each month for Umrah or Hajj?

Work backwards from the total cost you have researched and the date you want to travel. Divide the remaining amount by the number of months left. The cost depends on the season, the package and where you fly from, so use a current quote rather than a rule of thumb.

What if I miss a month?

Nothing breaks. Add the missed amount to the remaining total and recalculate: the monthly figure rises a little for the months that are left. The app does this automatically when you record what you have actually saved.

Should I keep goal money separate from my spending money?

Many people find it easier to. Budget Tracker records goal savings on their own and does not move money or change your account balances, so keep the actual money wherever you normally save and use the goal as the record of progress.
