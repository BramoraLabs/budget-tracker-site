A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
get started.

Is Budget Tracker free?

Yes. Accounts, spending, budgets, currencies, remittance tracking and the Zakat calculator are free. Pro adds exports and PDF reports through a one-time purchase.

Can I use it without internet?

Everyday financial tracking works offline. An internet connection is needed for store purchases and restoring purchases.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You record entries yourself, without providing bank credentials or connecting a bank account.

Does the app transfer money?

No. The remittance feature records transfers you make elsewhere, including exchange rates and fees. It does not move money.

Where can I get help?

Visit contact & support or explore our budgeting guides.
