A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
get started.

Is Budget Tracker free?

Yes. Accounts, spending, budgets, currencies, remittance tracking, the Zakat calculator, home-screen widgets, encrypted backups and up to two savings goals are free. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the goal limit and adds CSV/Excel exports and monthly PDF reports.

Does it have home-screen widgets?

Yes, on Android. You can add a spending widget and a savings-goal widget, and you can hide amounts on them. Widgets can show your figures on the home screen even when the app is locked, so use the hide-amounts setting if others use your phone. How to add widgets ↗

Can I use it without internet?

Everyday financial tracking works offline. An internet connection is needed for store purchases and restoring purchases.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You record entries yourself, without providing bank credentials or connecting a bank account.

Does the app transfer money?

No. The remittance feature records transfers you make elsewhere, including exchange rates and fees. It does not move money.

How do I delete my data?

Your data is stored on your phone. Deleting entries in the app, clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes it. Backup and export files you created stay wherever you saved them. The details, including purchase-related data, are in the app privacy policy.
