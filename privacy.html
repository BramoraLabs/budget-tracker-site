Website Privacy Policy

Operated by Bramora Labs  ·  Version 1.0  ·  August 30, 2026

This policy covers this website — the marketing pages you're reading right now. It is separate from the Budget Tracker Android app's privacy policy, which covers your financial data inside the app, including its limited RevenueCat purchase handling. See the app's Privacy Policy for that. This document was written to describe our practices honestly and in plain language, but it isn't a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer — if you need a legally certain answer, especially about a specific jurisdiction, ask one.

1. The short version

This website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts, and does not set cookies. Just browsing this site doesn't send us anything. The site has no sign-up form. The only personal information we hold is whatever you choose to email us.

2. The earlier "Get notified at launch" box

Before Budget Tracker launched on Google Play, this site had a box where you could leave your email address to be told when the app launched. That box has been removed. Submissions went through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service operated by Web3Creative, which forwarded each one as a plain email to our support inbox (bramoralabsupport@gmail.com). Emails received that way remain in that inbox, and Web3Forms may keep a copy under its own privacy policy, which we do not control. We have not used those addresses for anything other than the one-time launch note you asked for, plus occasional updates for people who ticked the separate optional box. We will not sell, rent or share them.

To ask us to delete your email, or to stop any updates, email bramoralabsupport@gmail.com at any time and we will remove it from our inbox and list. We cannot delete copies held by Web3Forms ourselves; contact them using the link above.

3. Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts — nothing from Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar. The small script that runs the scroll-fade animation on this site only reads the page's own layout; it doesn't collect or transmit anything. An older version of the removed signup box saved a small flag in your browser's local storage (not a cookie). It never left your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

4. Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft). Like most web hosts, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses at the infrastructure level for security and legal-compliance purposes — this is standard server access logging, not something Bramora Labs requests, collects, or has access to. See GitHub Pages' own data collection notice for details.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may give you rights over personal data a company holds about you — for example, to know what's held, correct it, delete it, or object to its use. Right now, the only personal information we hold about you is whatever you've chosen to email us, including any email you left in the earlier launch box, exactly like any other email you send someone. Reaching out about any of this is as simple as emailing bramoralabsupport@gmail.com. We haven't determined which of these specific rights formally apply to a business our size and haven't had this reviewed by a lawyer — if that matters to you, ask us and we'll do our best to help regardless.

6. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

7. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy whenever what the site actually does changes — for example, if we ever move to an automated email platform, add analytics, or anything else.

8. Legal applicability

This policy does not select a governing jurisdiction. The privacy rights and obligations that apply depend on mandatory law. This hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer, and if you're relying on this for a specific legal question, get independent advice.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: bramoralabsupport@gmail.com
