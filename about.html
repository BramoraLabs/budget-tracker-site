IT STARTS WITH SOMETHING SMALL

A problem you know.
An idea you care about.

Our first app, Budget Tracker, grew around a familiar challenge: managing everyday spending while earning in one currency and supporting family in another.

That means thinking about the things a generic money app can miss. Remittances. GCC currencies. Zakat. The comfort of keeping your financial records on your own device.

That same attention guides the studio: understand the problem, keep the experience clear, and keep making it better.
